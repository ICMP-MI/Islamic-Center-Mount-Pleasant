Connecting with Campus

If you're a CMU student, the Muslim Students Association (MSA) at CMU is the best first stop for campus-specific questions — things like on-campus prayer space or halal dining options are best confirmed directly with them. You can also follow them on Instagram.

Stay Connected

The easiest way to plug into the community, ask questions, and hear about events is our WhatsApp group. Fill out this form to join.

Daily Attendance

One of the simplest and most valuable ways to support the masjid is showing up. Join us for any of the five daily prayers and help keep our congregation strong.
